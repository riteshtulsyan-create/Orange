06

Consolidated Action Plan

Priority, what was found, and the recommended action. Full basis/justification for each is in the Excel workbook's "Action Plan" sheet.
High
Orange O Tec (Surat + Noida)
Combined entity made a Rs. 1.8 Cr net loss this quarter — a Rs. 6.4 Cr Surat loss only partly offset by a Rs. 4.6 Cr Noida profit.
Action: Standalone review of Surat's pricing, cost allocation, and branch/related-party transactions within 2 weeks.
High
Machines — Receivables
94% of what Machines customers owe (Rs. 32.3 Cr of Rs. 34.2 Cr) is overdue beyond 180 days — the worst collection quality of any segment.
Action: Freeze dispatch to customers overdue >180 days; recover at least 25% of the aged balance (~Rs. 8 Cr) by quarter-end.
High
Heads segment
Gross profit % collapsed from 15.0% to 4.5% year-on-year, on a sales base that also fell 26%.
Action: Review pricing/discount limits this month; confirm whether the vanished "OTHER" head category (Rs. 5.2 Cr of GP last year) was lost or reclassified.
Medium
Direct Expenses
Up 83% year-on-year and 46% above the normal quarterly run-rate — elevated on both measures. Custom Duty alone is up 55%.
Action: Line-by-line finance review of Direct Expenses and Custom Duty before the quarter closes.
Medium
Pigment Ink
Gross margin fell from 30.4% to 9.2% year-on-year — the steepest drop of any ink line.
Action: Review input cost and selling price directly with product/pricing — a drop this steep usually means a cost increase hasn't been passed through.
Medium
Ink — Working Capital
Net working capital rose Rs. 5.7 Cr, the largest segment increase, with 72% of receivables also overdue beyond 180 days.
Action: Recover 30% of aged receivables (~Rs. 8 Cr) by quarter-end; review inventory ordering against actual demand.
Medium
Debtor Days vs Creditor Days
Collections take ~96 days, payments to suppliers ~90 — funding customers at the company's own expense. Both are better than the FY25-26 average, though.
Action: Target Debtor Days ≤ 80 over two quarters via tighter credit terms and faster follow-up.
Medium
Machine Sales — softening demand
Revenue down 13.4%, quantity down 6.7% year-on-year — a segment with no visibility at all in the standard GP report.
Action: Short review with the Machines sales team on next quarter's order pipeline; don't extrapolate from May's one-off spike.
Low
Spare Parts — quantity spike
Sale quantity jumped from ~483 to ~10,667 units between May and June — looks like a data/unit-of-measure issue rather than genuine demand.
Action: Verify against source records before using in any external report or forecast.
